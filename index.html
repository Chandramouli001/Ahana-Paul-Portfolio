About the Artistकलाकार

Art, for me, is a form of prayer. My devotional paintings of Ganesha, Kali, Hanuman and Krishna pour out in vivid colour, while my portraits stay quiet — black graphite, soft light, honest faces.

Whether it's a deity for your home mandir or a portrait of someone you love, each piece is drawn with patience and care.
